## Description (≤260 chars)
MCPWP turns any WordPress site into an MCP server. Claude, Cursor, or any AI client can manage your content, Elementor layouts, media, and SEO through natural language — with an approval queue so nothing ships without your OK.

## First Comment (story / context)
Hey PH 👋

We built MCPWP after watching agency teams spend hours copy-pasting content between Claude and WordPress. The manual loop was the problem — not the AI.

MCPWP installs as a WordPress plugin, generates an API key, and from that point your AI assistant has direct access to your site through MCP. It can write a post, build an Elementor page, audit your SEO, and upload media — all in one session, no switching tabs.

The part we're most proud of: the approval queue. Every change the AI proposes can be gated behind a human review. You see a diff, approve or reject, and if something goes wrong there's a one-click rollback. AI writes fast, you stay in control.

**What's free:** All content tools, Elementor editing, media, menus, approvals, webhooks.
**What's Pro:** SEO audit + autofix, staging push, WooCommerce tools.

Would love to hear from anyone using Claude or Cursor with WordPress. What's your current workflow?
