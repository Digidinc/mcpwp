# Video Distribution

Distribution packages adapt an approved master video to a specific channel.
They do not grant publishing authority.

## Package contents

- approved render and digest;
- channel, account, aspect ratio, and duration;
- title, description, caption, thumbnail, and alt text;
- links with campaign parameters;
- affiliate, sponsorship, or course disclosure;
- intended publish window;
- human publisher and reviewer;
- PostHog or platform measurement identifier.

## Channel rules

- Re-check character limits and media requirements at packaging time.
- Do not reuse outdated pricing, feature counts, compatibility claims, or
  security wording from an older channel draft.
- Keep a separate package for YouTube, Shorts, X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Medium, and
  course surfaces when their copy or disclosure differs.
- Publishing, scheduling, replies, outreach, and paid promotion require an
  explicit human action or approved workflow gate.

## Completion

A distribution package is ready when the render, copy, links, disclosures, and
review verdict agree. Publication is complete only when its receipt records the
live destination and observed publication state.
