# OBS Recording Specifications

OBS is the human-controlled capture surface for real product demonstrations,
live walkthroughs, voice, and camera footage.

## Repository boundary

Store scene specifications and capture checklists here. Do not commit:

- OBS user profiles or machine-specific configuration;
- stream keys, account tokens, cookies, or browser-session data;
- unreviewed recordings containing private dashboards or customer information;
- absolute local media paths.

## Scene specification

Each recording brief should define:

- canvas and output resolution;
- frame rate and recording format;
- named scenes and source order;
- microphone and system-audio expectations;
- safe crop regions for credentials and notifications;
- a clean demonstration account or fixture;
- start-state and reset instructions;
- the human who controls record, pause, and stop.

## Control rule

Lumen may inspect and propose OBS scenes. Lumen controls OBS only when the user
explicitly requests that action and remains responsible for the recording
session. Starting a recording is never inferred from approval of a script.
